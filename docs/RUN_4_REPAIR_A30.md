This repair responds to both returned a29 cases, Treppenhaus Sued and EG Kita.

proposals now feed a separate original-point geometry validator. Resulting model

## Adapter identity and limitations

The supplied graph/companion hashes and model-pack digest remain unchanged.
The descriptor adds adapter `a30-streaming-core-voxel-normal-v2`; implementation
hashes invalidate old caches. Representative-only normals and the declared
centering order are a preprocessing revision, not a claim of native-training
parity. The exact 40-tensor graph contract is preserved.

The generated fixtures test transport, original-evidence validation and negative
cases. They do not prove model precision/recall, improve a particular room, or
guarantee a target runtime on the designated computer. No new supplied-weight
end-to-end execution was available in this repair environment.

Remaining limits include the explicit total view/tile budget, unresolved native
B subtype meanings, full beam/column acceptance, broad roof/ramp recognition and
the planned viewer infrastructure/presentation work. Surface remnants that cannot
be represented without an overlap or an unvalidated cut remain proposals.
